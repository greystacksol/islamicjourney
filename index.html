Islamic Journey

Walk with the Prophet ﷺ from his birth in Makkah to his passing in Madinah, and on through the Khilafah of the four Rightly Guided Caliphs. Every moment is linked to the ayat revealed, the people who were there and the hadith that record it.

Mufti Afzal Hoosen Elias), with the grade of the source it cites. Journey stops and ghazawat are taken from the "Safar-e-Zindagi" map video by @aamirrauf1.0. That video dates the birth to 571 CE; here years before prophethood follow a 570 CE birth and the standard ages. Skeleton with sample data. Dates are approximate (scholars differ) and English meanings are short paraphrases. Quran text and English/Urdu translations come from QuranEnc via the open quran-json dataset (CC BY 4.0). Mufti Taqi Usmani's translation and Ma'ariful Qur'an are copyrighted, so they are linked on quran.com rather than copied here. Nasab Nama · Lineage

The agreed lineage from Adnan to the Prophet ﷺ. His father's and mother's lines meet at Kilab. Lineage beyond Adnan back to Isma'il (a.s.) is not agreed upon, so it is left out here.
